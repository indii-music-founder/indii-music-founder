# Emergency Break-Glass Access Procedure

## 1. Conditions for Activation
Break-glass access may be activated ONLY when standard access pathways are blocked during an active P0 incident (e.g. primary administrator account locked out, emergency credential rotation).

## 2. Procedure
1. **Notification**: Incident Commander announces break-glass activation in the emergency communications channel.
2. **Access Issuance**: Temporary elevated permissions are granted to the designated engineer via GCP IAM console.
3. **Action Execution**: Specific corrective actions are executed and recorded in the incident log.
4. **Immediate Deactivation**: Within **2 hours** of incident resolution, the elevated permission is revoked.
5. **Post-Incident Audit**: Full Cloud Audit Logs from the break-glass window are extracted, reviewed by the Founder, and appended to the incident postmortem.
