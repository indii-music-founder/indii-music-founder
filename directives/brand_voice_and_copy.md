# Brand Voice & Copywriting Directive

**Status:** Active
**Target:** All UI copy, marketing pages, email communications, and user-facing text.

## The Core Philosophy
indii.music is an operating system and a virtual team for independent musicians. Our target audience is artists who want to focus on music while we handle the heavy lifting.

**Musicians hate corporate tech-speak, they hate pretentious AI hype, and they view "AI" as an entity trying to replace them.** 

Our copy must sound like a human explaining a tool to a friend at a bar. It must be direct, grounded, and stripped of all neon cyberpunk buzzwords.

## The Absolute Rules

1. **NO "AI" MENTIONS:** The word "AI" (Artificial Intelligence) is strictly banned from all user-facing copy. It triggers anxiety about job replacement in the music industry. Use words like "Automated," "Virtual Team," or "Advanced Reasoning Engine" instead.
2. **NO THIRD-PARTY BRANDING:** Never mention the underlying technology models or open-source protocols (e.g., Gemini, VEO, DDEX). This is our proprietary blend. Do not show the plumbing.
3. **NO "WAR ROOM" AGGRESSION:** Avoid militaristic or aggressive corporate speak (e.g., "Total Operational Supremacy," "Independent Command," "Engagement Swarm"). We are a support network, not a defense contractor.
4. **NO TECH-SPIRITUALISM:** Avoid mystical jargon applied to software (e.g., "Visual Alchemist," "Sonic DNA," "Life Force," "Soul Certified").
5. **NO QUALIFIERS FOR THE TEAM:** When referring to the 15-piece virtual team, drop words like "virtual" or "digital." Just call them "Your team" or "Your specialists."
