# Personnel and Contractor Security Policy (POL-015)

## 1. Purpose
To ensure that all individuals working with indii.music are vetted, trained, understand their security responsibilities, and protect proprietary intellectual property.

## 2. Onboarding Requirements
- **Confidentiality Agreement**: All team members and contractors must sign a non-disclosure agreement (NDA) and IP assignment before receiving repository or cloud access.
- **Access Provisioning**: Access is provisioned strictly on a least-privilege, need-to-know basis.
- **Security Awareness**: New team members receive security briefing on credential safety, phishing, and the zero-commit secret policy.

## 3. Ongoing Requirements
- Annual security refresher training on social engineering, credential hygiene, and incident reporting.
- Compliance with the Acceptable Use Policy (POL-013).

## 4. Offboarding
- Immediate revocation of all access (GitHub, GCP, Firebase, Slack, email) within 8 hours of separation.
- Written acknowledgment of ongoing confidentiality obligations.
