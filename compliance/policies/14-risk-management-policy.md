# Risk Management Policy (POL-014)

## 1. Purpose
To establish a structured methodology for identifying, assessing, mitigating, and reviewing technical, operational, and organizational risks across indii.music.

## 2. Risk Assessment Process
1. **Identification**: Engineering and leadership identify threats against assets (code, audio vault, credentials, user privacy).
2. **Analysis & Scoring**: Risks are evaluated using a standard Likelihood x Impact matrix (1-5 scale) to calculate an overall Risk Score (1-25).
3. **Treatment Decision**: Each identified risk is assigned a mitigation strategy:
   - *Mitigate*: Implement engineering or operational controls.
   - *Accept*: Formally accept residual risk with leadership signoff.
   - *Avoid*: Redesign the feature to eliminate exposure.
   - *Transfer*: Utilize third-party insurance or cloud provider SLAs.
4. **Monitoring**: The Risk Register (`compliance/risk/risk-register.md`) is reviewed and updated at least annually.
