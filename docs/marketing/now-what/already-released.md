## Educational article — ready for editorial review

# You released through DistroKid. Now what?

Finishing one milestone creates a new business question. You do not need to solve your whole career today. Start with a small inventory of where you are and decide which task deserves attention first.

## Review your records

Compare your release titles, credits, identifiers, and links with your distributor records. Keep the details somewhere you can revisit and share with the people working on the project. Mark what you know and what still needs confirmation.

## Review collection coverage

Distribution and rights administration involve different jobs. Check which organizations and agreements apply to your music. Separate completed work from assumptions. An account, an upload, or a promise from a service does not by itself confirm every part of your music business is covered.

## Plan the next campaign

Choose one song, one audience, and a sustainable posting schedule. Give that task a clear owner and completion date. Then review the result before deciding what comes next.

Artist Passport asks about your stage, rights setup, distribution, and priorities, and provides an initial business roadmap. Its guidance is based on your answers; it does not independently verify registrations, rights, earnings, or eligibility.
