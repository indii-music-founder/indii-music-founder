## Educational article — ready for editorial review

# Your music is making money. Now what?

Finishing one milestone creates a new business question. You do not need to solve your whole career today. Start with a small inventory of where you are and decide which task deserves attention first.

## Gather your income records

Collect distributor statements, show receipts, merchandise sales, and licensing records. Keep the details somewhere you can revisit and share with the people working on the project. Mark what you know and what still needs confirmation.

## Record the costs

Organize costs by release and month so you can see what each activity contributes. Separate completed work from assumptions. An account, an upload, or a promise from a service does not by itself confirm every part of your music business is covered.

## Agree collaborator obligations

Document shares and recoupment terms. Check the numbers before scheduling payments. Give that task a clear owner and completion date. Then review the result before deciding what comes next.
