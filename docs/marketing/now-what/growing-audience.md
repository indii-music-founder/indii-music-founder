## Educational article — ready for editorial review

# People are listening. Now what?

Finishing one milestone creates a new business question. You do not need to solve your whole career today. Start with a small inventory of where you are and decide which task deserves attention first.

## Give listeners a next step

Offer a clear invitation to follow, join a mailing list with consent, or attend a show. Keep the details somewhere you can revisit and share with the people working on the project. Mark what you know and what still needs confirmation.

## Choose a repeatable format

Make a content series you can sustain instead of restarting every week. Separate completed work from assumptions. An account, an upload, or a promise from a service does not by itself confirm every part of your music business is covered.

## Measure connection

Track replies, signups, and returning engagement alongside plays. Give that task a clear owner and completion date. Then review the result before deciding what comes next.
