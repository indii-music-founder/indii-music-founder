## Educational article — ready for editorial review

# You finished your first song. Now what?

Finishing one milestone creates a new business question. You do not need to solve your whole career today. Start with a small inventory of where you are and decide which task deserves attention first.

## Record the credits

Write down the writers, performers, producers, and everyone’s agreed responsibilities. Keep the details somewhere you can revisit and share with the people working on the project. Mark what you know and what still needs confirmation.

## Prepare the release

Gather the finished audio, artwork, credits, and a realistic release date. Separate completed work from assumptions. An account, an upload, or a promise from a service does not by itself confirm every part of your music business is covered.

## Choose one next task

Start with the missing piece you can finish this week. Give that task a clear owner and completion date. Then review the result before deciding what comes next.
