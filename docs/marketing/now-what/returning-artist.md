## Educational article — ready for editorial review

# You’re returning to music. Now what?

Finishing one milestone creates a new business question. You do not need to solve your whole career today. Start with a small inventory of where you are and decide which task deserves attention first.

## Take inventory

List the recordings, accounts, agreements, and assets you already have. Keep the details somewhere you can revisit and share with the people working on the project. Mark what you know and what still needs confirmation.

## Check what changed

Review your contact details, catalog credits, distributor access, and current agreements. Separate completed work from assumptions. An account, an upload, or a promise from a service does not by itself confirm every part of your music business is covered.

## Build a manageable restart

Choose one release or project and one achievable weekly business task. Give that task a clear owner and completion date. Then review the result before deciding what comes next.
