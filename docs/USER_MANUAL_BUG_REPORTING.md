# Reporting Bugs & Feature Requests

indii makes it easy to report problems and request features. Your feedback goes directly to your agent team for immediate action.

## Quick Start

**Report a Bug:** Press `Ctrl+Shift+B` (Windows/Linux) or `Cmd+Shift+B` (Mac)

**Request a Feature:** Press `Ctrl+Shift+F` (Windows/Linux) or `Cmd+Shift+F` (Mac)

A dialog opens where you describe your issue or idea. Click "Send to Agent" and your report goes straight to your current agent.

---

## Reporting a Bug

When something doesn't work the way you expect:

1. **Press `Ctrl+Shift+B`** to open the bug report dialog
2. **Fill in:**
   - **What broke?** — A short title (e.g., "Image generation crashes on mobile")
   - **Description** — What you were trying to do
   - **Steps to Reproduce** — How to trigger the problem (optional but helpful)
   - **Expected Behavior** — What should have happened
   - **Actual Behavior** — What actually happened
   - **Severity** — How bad is it?
     - 🔴 **Critical** — Blocks you from using the app
     - 🟠 **Major** — Significant impact on your workflow
     - 🟡 **Minor** — Workaround exists
     - 🔵 **Cosmetic** — Visual issue only
3. **Click "Send to Agent"**

Your agent receives the report and can:
- Fix it immediately
- Ask you follow-up questions
- Escalate to other specialists
- Track progress in your bug log

---

## Requesting a Feature

When you think of something that would make indii better:

1. **Press `Ctrl+Shift+F`** to open the feature request dialog
2. **Fill in:**
   - **Feature title** — What's your idea? (e.g., "Add dark mode toggle")
   - **Description** — Why would this be useful?
   - **Use Case** — How would you use it? What problem does it solve?
   - **Priority** — How important is this to you?
     - **Critical** — Would solve a major pain point
     - **Important** — Would improve your workflow significantly
     - **Nice to have** — Cool to have but not essential
   - **Category** — Which area?
     - **UX / Interface** — How things look or work
     - **Performance** — Speed or efficiency
     - **Integration** — Connecting with other tools
     - **Content** — More/better data
     - **Other** — Something else
3. **Click "Send to Agent"**

Your agent reviews your idea and can:
- Prioritize it with other requests
- Discuss trade-offs with you
- Implement it
- Connect you with other users who want the same thing

## Conversational Reporting

You can also tell your agent directly:

**For bugs:**
- "I ran into a problem with image generation. Can you file a bug report?"
- "This feature isn't working. I need to report it."
- "Something crashed when I tried to..."

**For features:**
- "I have an idea. Would it be possible to...?"
- "It would be great if indii could..."
- "Can you request this feature for me?"

Your agent understands and will help you file the report or feature request.

---

## What Happens After You Report

1. **Immediate** — Your agent sees the report in real-time
2. **Logged** — The report is saved to indii's database
3. **Synced** — It's added to your project's GitHub issues (for tracking)
4. **Actionable** — Your agent can:
   - Fix bugs right away
   - Schedule feature work
   - Ask clarifying questions
   - Keep you in the loop

---

## Tips for Better Reports

**Bugs:**
- Include exact steps to reproduce (helps your agent fix it faster)
- Be specific about what went wrong (error message, expected result, actual result)
- Mention the device/browser if relevant (mobile vs desktop, Chrome vs Safari)

**Features:**
- Explain the *why*, not just the *what* (problem you're solving)
- Describe your ideal workflow
- Mention if you've seen this in other tools
