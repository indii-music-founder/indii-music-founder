# Video Concept: "The Ultimate Stack"

**Audio:** The sound of a busy, chaotic deli or kitchen. Plates clinking, people talking fast, cash registers. A low, tense baseline throbs underneath.

## Scene 1: The Spread (The Fragmented Industry)
*   **Visual:** A long, cinematic tracking shot across a pristine stainless steel counter. Lined up are 20 different, small, single-ingredient sandwiches. One is just a slice of cheese. One is just a single piece of lettuce. One is just turkey. They look inadequate on their own.
*   **Voiceover:** "You want to be an independent artist."

## Scene 2: The realization
*   **Visual:** The camera stops at the end of the line. A pair of hands enters the frame. They pause, hovering over the pathetic, fragmented sandwiches.
*   **Voiceover:** "But independence shouldn't mean trying to survive on crumbs."

## Scene 3: The Assembly (The Consolidation)
*   **Visual:** The hands go to work. Fast, rhythmic cuts. Instead of picking one sandwich, the hands start grabbing all of them. Slapping the cheese on the turkey, the lettuce on the tomato. The stack grows higher and higher, faster and faster, becoming a massive, towering, epic masterpiece of a sandwich. The deli noise builds to a crescendo.
*   **Voiceover:** "All of the things..."

## Scene 4: The Anchor (The Logo Transition)
*   **Visual:** The hands grab two thick, heavy bamboo sandwich skewers.
*   **Audio:** The deli noise cuts out completely. Absolute silence.
*   **Visual:** With deliberate force, the hands slam the two skewers straight down through the top of the massive sandwich.
*   **Voiceover:** "...for all of the artists."

## Scene 5: The Reveal (The Brand)
*   **Audio:** A massive, cinematic bass drop hits the exact moment the skewers pierce the bread.
*   **Visual:** The moment the skewers hit, the image morphs. The two skewers instantly become the bright cyan vertical lines of the ii logo. The massive sandwich disappears, replaced by the glowing, rainbow rounded-square halo surrounding the ii, glowing intensely in the dark.
*   **Voiceover:** "Life is your art. Art is your business. This is indii."

## Scene 6: The Call to Action
*   **Visual:** The glowing logo holds on screen. Elegant text fades in beneath it.
*   **Text:** 
    The Founder Waitlist is Open. 
    founder.indii.music.
