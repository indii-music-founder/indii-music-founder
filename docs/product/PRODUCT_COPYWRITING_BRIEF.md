## 1. The Core Narrative

**indii** is an operating system and dedicated virtual team for independent musicians. It picks up where music mastering ends.

### The Artist Pain Point
Independent artists are overwhelmed by the business side of music: contracts, split sheets, DDEX distribution metadata, press pitches, visual branding, social rollout schedules, and tour routing. When they try to use generic automated tools or chatbots, they get generic advice that forgets who they are, hallucinates copyright law, and produces generic artwork that damages their brand.

### The indii Breakthrough: Two Living Innovations

1. **A Dedicated Team of 23 Specialists (Not a Generic Bot):**
   Instead of one confused chatbot trying to do everything, `indii` gives the artist a full team of 23 domain specialists—from legal and licensing to brand management, mastering analysis, and direct distribution. Each specialist knows only their domain and executes with professional precision.

2. **The Living Master Directive (Supreme Law on Your Terms):**
   Artists don't trust black boxes. In `indii`, the artist has a **Master Directive**—a living playbook in Studio Settings. You set your sound profile (target LUFS, sample rates), your legal red lines (minimum split baselines, audit clauses), and your visual aesthetic (palette, styling). Your entire team of 23 specialists strictly obeys your playbook.

3. **Instant Visual & Operational Cascading:**
   When you change your brand colors or style in your Master Directive, that data immediately cascades throughout the entire application—restyling your interface, updating your campaign visuals, and instructing your design specialists automatically. You never have to re-explain yourself.

## 2. Brand Voice & Language Rules (Mandatory)

All copywriters and campaign agents must follow these non-negotiable rules from `directives/brand_voice_and_copy.md`:

| Rule | Requirement | Why |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Lowercase Marks** | Always write `indii`, `indii.music`, and `wiil` in lowercase. Never capitalize. | Core visual and typographical identity. |
| **No "AI" Mentions** | Do not use the word "AI" or "Artificial Intelligence" in user-facing copy. | Musicians fear job displacement; focus on the outcome, not the tech. |
| **Use "Your Team"** | Call the 23 agents "Your team", "Your specialists", or "Your crew". | Sounds like a real support network, not cold software. |
| **No Tech-Spiritualism** | Ban words like "Visual Alchemist", "Sonic Life Force", "Neural Cortex". | Sounds like pretentious hype; speak like a friend at a studio session. |
| **No Corporate Aggression** | Ban words like "Total Operational Supremacy" or "War Room". | We are a supportive partner for creatives, not a defense contractor. |

## 4. Plug-and-Play Copy Assets

### A. 30-Second Elevator Pitch
> *"Every major label artist has a team of 20 people handling their contracts, distribution, visual campaigns, and tour routing. Independent artists have had to do all of it alone—until now. indii gives you a dedicated team of 23 specialists that run your music business on your terms. You set your studio rules, sound standards, and brand style in your Master Directive, and your team executes everything around the clock."*

### B. Hero Headlines & Subheads (Website & Landing Page)

**Option 1 (Control & Independence):**
- **Headline:** Your music. Your rules. A dedicated team to run the business.
- **Subhead:** Set your sound standards, brand identity, and contract rules once. Let your 23-piece team handle the distribution, marketing, and legal work.

**Option 2 (The Anti-Generic Angle):**
- **Headline:** Stop explaining your brand to generic software.
- **Subhead:** With indii's living Master Directive, your team learns your aesthetic, respects your sound profile, and runs your release campaigns with zero guesswork.

**Option 3 (Team-First):**
- **Headline:** The team behind independent artists who refuse to slow down.
- **Subhead:** From contract checks and DSP distribution to custom video production and brand sync, indii handles the business so you can stay in the music.

### C. Feature-to-Benefit Value Points

1. **The Living Master Directive:**
   - *Feature:* A co-authored Markdown playbook in settings that overrides all system defaults.
   - *Artist Benefit:* You stay in control. Define your minimum royalty splits, target loudness, and release schedule once; your team never deviates from your standards.
2. **Instant Brand Sync (Visual DNA):**
   - *Feature:* Hex codes and aesthetic styling cascade to CSS, DOM, and creative prompts simultaneously.
   - *Artist Benefit:* Update your colors once, and your entire studio, social teasers, and merchandise concepts update automatically in seconds.
3. **23 Specialized Team Members:**
   - *Feature:* Discrete, domain-isolated agents for Legal, Finance, Touring, Marketing, and Music.
   - *Artist Benefit:* You get real domain expertise. When you ask a legal question, a legal specialist answers—not a general chatbot guessing at copyright law.
4. **Direct Distribution Engine:**
   - *Feature:* Native DDEX ERN packaging with lossless audio delivery.
   - *Artist Benefit:* Keep 100% of your rights and deliver directly to DSPs with zero middleman friction.
