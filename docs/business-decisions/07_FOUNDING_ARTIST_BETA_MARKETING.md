## Website and conversion flow

All owned public aliases, including `indii.music` and `www.indii.music`, should resolve to one canonical public landing page. Canonical URLs, redirects, metadata, social cards, structured data, sitemaps, and authentication return URLs must agree.

The public root stays the front door for everyone. A signed-out member visiting `indii.music` sees the same public landing page as a new visitor. A signed-in member may see an **Open app** action, but should not be automatically pulled away from the marketing site. The protected Studio lives at `app.indii.music`; an unauthenticated Studio visit enters authentication and returns to the Studio after success.

The current website does not need a wholesale redesign. Preserve its strongest visual work and story sections while updating copy, truthful capability boundaries, calls to action, and product paths.

## Public pricing

| Stage | Temporary monthly price | Intended artist                                                 |
| ----- | ----------------------: | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Free  |                      $0 | Verified artist completing one bounded guided creative proof    |
| Start |                     $22 | Artist beginning to organize and operate a career               |
| Build |                     $55 | Artist actively releasing and building repeatable operations    |
| Scale |                    $110 | Artist with an active career, larger workload, and music income |

Free, Start, Build, and Scale are the approved public names. Prices are introductory and may change after beta when provider and operating costs are measured. Show every current price publicly while the waitlist is open.

Plans increase both capacity and stage-appropriate capability. Each plan should feel complete for its intended stage. Do not manufacture a dead end that forces an upgrade just to finish one task.

Offer monthly, quarterly, six-month, and annual billing. Commitment discounts are approximately 5%, 10%, and 20% respectively. Show the total charged and monthly equivalent. Use honest whole-number base prices and never `.99` charm pricing. Final rounded totals require margin and checkout validation.

## Extra capacity and microtransactions

When included usage runs out, offer the appropriate combination of:

- a small unit purchase;
- a workflow/project pack;
- a larger reusable capacity pack.

Purchased extra capacity **never expires**. Recommend upgrading when repeated top-ups make a higher plan sensible, but always let the artist choose. Artists must be able to finish a project without taking on an unnecessary subscription.

## Verified free experience

The interactive website demonstration and free creative experience are one flow:

1. Visitor selects **See how indii.music works**.
2. Before the demo, the visitor verifies an email-only account, which joins the beta waitlist.
3. The artist uploads finished music and an image, or creates the starting image in the allowed path.
4. indii guides the artist from upload through a coordinated mini campaign.
5. The artist exports a mini campaign pack of images and short clips.
6. A practical target is several approximately eight-second clips with the artist's music plus matching images.
7. Exports contain no indii watermark or forced branding.
8. The artist chooses whether to save or delete uploads and resulting project assets.

This is a bounded useful proof—not a blank viewer and not an unlimited credit giveaway.

## Product video system

The first set is eight 15–30 second clips, one for each lifecycle stage: Finished music, Plan, Register, Prepare delivery, Campaign, Release, Track, Repeat.

Each clip appears within its timeline stage, begins only after the visitor clicks play, and always has captions available. The default format is wiil's founder voice over real product capture. On-camera or artist-use footage may be tested later but is not required initially.

Replace the simulated showcase progressively with a guided interactive demonstration and real capture. Until then, simulations must be labeled illustrative.
