## Draft answers

### What are you building?

indii.music is business operating software for independent music artists. It connects the work around finished music — planning, rights and registrations, delivery preparation, creative campaigns, release operations, finance, audience/CRM, merchandise, touring, and repeatable business workflows — around shared artist and project context.

The lifecycle is:

Finished Music → Plan → Register → Prepare Delivery → Campaign → Release → Track → Repeat.

### What problem are you solving?

Independent artists increasingly operate like small music companies, but the business work around music is fragmented across distributors, rights organizations, spreadsheets, accounting tools, creative tools, social platforms, calendars, email, and outside services.

Each tool may solve one problem, but context is lost between them. That fragmentation causes missed registrations, duplicated work, incomplete records, weak financial visibility, and unnecessary outsourcing.

### Why now?

Independent artists carry more label, management, administration, marketing, and operating responsibility than ever. At the same time, modern AI, cloud infrastructure, and music-data standards make it possible for one connected system to help an artist operate across those workflows without requiring a large team.

### What is different?

The differentiation is continuity, not feature count.

The same artist, song, release, rights, collaborator, campaign, creative asset, audience, and financial context can be used across the system. Specialist AI works inside bounded departments, while deterministic software remains authoritative for exact calculations, identifiers, permissions, security, and workflow state.

The product also has persistent directive/control layers, Boardroom/Department/Direct operating modes, product-native diagnostics, and a live bug-reporting path that can create structured GitHub engineering issues from inside the product.

### Why are you the founder for this?

I have spent roughly four decades working across music and operations. I have been a DJ, promoter, event producer, nightclub operator, producer, label owner, mastering engineer, restaurant operator, manager, and opening-team member.

Through Expressway Records I personally handled the operating chain around releases: artists, metadata, registrations, distribution preparation, promotion, accounting/royalty work, and final mastering. I also completed a two-year Music Business program at SAE Institute Nashville.

Since November 2025 I have worked full-time on indii.music, directing the product, domain architecture, technical decisions, AI-agent workflows, testing standards, review, and delivery proof.

### How much have you built?

Working software exists across web, desktop, and cloud surfaces. The platform includes rights/registration workflows, DDEX/distribution-preparation infrastructure, finance/splits/expenses, CRM/social systems, merchandise, creative image/video workflows, remote phone-to-Studio execution, internal operations tooling, and specialist-agent orchestration.

The repository shows sustained daily development since late November 2025, extensive automated tests and CI, and a founder-designed development harness that requires evidence rather than accepting agent claims of completion.

### What is your stage?

Working software entering founder-led beta validation. The core platform exists. Current work is production hardening, completing external/service connections, real-user onboarding, first commercial adoption, and turning intensive founder-led development into a repeatable operating company.

### What traction do you have?

Pre-revenue. No paying customers and no confirmed active external beta users yet. A small waitlist exists.

The strongest proof today is product execution and development velocity. The next proof is market validation: real independent artists using important workflows, returning, identifying measurable value, and paying.

### How do you use AI?

AI is both part of the product and part of the development/operating system.

The product uses bounded specialist agents inside explicit control layers. The company uses AI-assisted engineering inside founder-designed workflows with tests, CI, exact-SHA verification, error ledgers, approval gates, and deterministic fallbacks.

A current internal architecture initiative is a Living Execution Graph that will connect work provenance, dependencies, GitHub issues/PRs/commits, discovered child work, convergence, and completion evidence.

The goal is an event-driven operating loop:

Sense → Understand → Decide → Execute → Verify → Learn.

### What is your business model?

Current beta packaging includes subscription tiers plus a limited Founding Owner license. Exact current public pricing should be copied from the live application/landing page at submission time rather than from stale notes.

### Who is the initial customer?

Self-managed independent artists and artist-producers with recurring releases and enough operating complexity that fragmented tools already cost them time, money, or missed administrative steps.

### How will you get customers?

Founder-led beta onboarding first. The immediate objective is to work directly with a small number of independent artists, observe activation and repeated use, identify the workflows that create measurable value, and use real product demonstrations and case evidence to refine positioning and acquisition before scaling paid marketing.

### Why PearX?

The product-building risk has been reduced substantially; the largest remaining company risk is market validation and go-to-market learning.

PearX is directly relevant because its published program emphasizes first customers, scalable sales motion, early hiring, fundraising, and founder learning. Those are the company's highest-leverage next problems.
