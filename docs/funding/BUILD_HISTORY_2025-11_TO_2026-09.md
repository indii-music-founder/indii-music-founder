# indii.music Build History — Nov. 2025 to Sep. 2026

**As of:** 2026-09-26  
**Purpose:** Evidence-backed product/engineering chronology for funding and diligence.

## November–December 2025 — from experiment to product foundation

The founder identifies November 2025 as the point when indii.music became the product rather than another experiment. Current main begins with an Initial commit on 2025-11-28, followed by the creative-studio seed, AI-service work, UI/architecture work, and domain tools.

The repo records 39 commits from Nov. 28–30 and 634 in December.

## January–February 2026 — broad application and intelligence layer

January shows rapid expansion across security, UI, creative workflows, tests, history, distribution/release authority, and stateful application behavior. February continues product hardening while model/platform and browser-agent work becomes more deliberate.

Current-main commit counts: January 2,241; February 629.

## March 2026 — remote relay and agent orchestration

Representative March history includes controller/remote routes, phone safety timeouts, server-side agent relay, orchestration routing, and specialist fine-tuning/evaluation datasets.

This is the point where the architecture clearly extends beyond a desktop app into phone → cloud relay → Studio/agent execution.

## April–May 2026 — architecture, routing, and specialist responsibility

April continues hardening and formal architecture/diligence work. Some acquisition-era documents created in this period were later corrected because they mixed scenarios/fixtures with current facts.

May shows explicit specialist routing and responsibility corrections, including which department owns identifiers such as ISRCs. Skills/routing are pruned and evaluated rather than simply accumulated.

Current-main commit counts: April 870; May 1,034.

## June–July 2026 — engineering operating system

June and July are dominated by issue-led remediation, testing, CI, security, runtime/deployment work, checkpoints, and evidence logging.

This period establishes the founder-created engineering harness now expressed through /start, /middle, /end, /ci-validate, exact-SHA proof, no-post-gate edits, error ledgers, bounded work, and deployment/security guards.

Current-main commit counts: June 2,096; July 1,340.

## August 2026 — production/commercial alignment

August includes cloud/deployment hardening plus increasing alignment of landing-page messaging, beta packaging, pricing, remote operations, and product truth.

Current Founding Artist Beta packaging emerges from this period: Free, Start $22, Build $55, Scale $110, and Founding Owner $2,500 one-time.

## September 2026 — connected business OS

September brings together the major operating layers.

### Social / Meta
PR #281 aligns Instagram publishing validation, Views/engagement ingestion, inbound-only DM rules, OAuth/webhook groundwork, CRM signals, and agent behavior. Genuine live Meta verification remains a separate gate.

### Canonical music / DDEX
PR #285 establishes canonical music relationships and a DDEX standards registry. Later phases add Connected Intelligence preflight, catalog-integrity analysis, RDR-RCC interoperability, scoped AI-use rights, local/off-grid inspection, and Founding Owner capability boundaries.

### Remote / field operations
indiiREMOTE now includes authenticated remote commands, approvals/status, voice/photo/document/receipt/video/location/text capture, Field Encounters, contacts/notes, and mileage/business context. A Sep. 26 audit found the durable media/contact structures are ahead of the analysis wiring: the current Gemini encounter request is text-only and does not yet attach captured audio/photo/video evidence. Issue #318 tracks that corrected media-analysis gap.

### Product-native repair loop
Conversational bug/error reporting, Firestore persistence, server-side GitHub credentials, deduplication, and founder-only triage exist. A Sep. 26 configuration fault left seven reports safely stored in Firestore but not forwarded to GitHub. Commit 5504361c9 repairs the route; issue #319 backfills preserved reports. A fresh post-repair automatic roundtrip is still the final live-verification step.

### Truth controls
Issue #317 tracks a Boardroom answer that overstated production readiness. The corrected contract requires status responses to distinguish implemented, tested, live-verified, blocked/degraded, and externally gated capabilities.

### Post-Mastering Administrative Engine
Sep. 26 also advances:
- P1: lifecycle FSM, task queue, ledger receipts, fixed-point shares;
- P2: deterministic catalog audit + Jev triage + durable task emission;
- P3: catalog-gap, registration-staging, and split-invitation tools;

P4.2 then extends the engine-side DSP analysis with EBU loudness measurement, key estimation, and BWF provenance. P1 has explicit successful deployment evidence after a transient provider 503 retry. P2–P4.2 have strong code/test evidence; production status should be checked at claim time.
