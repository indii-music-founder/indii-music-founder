## Create once and reuse

### Founder video

Prepare a clean 60–90 second founder video with:

1. name and company;
2. what indii.music does;
3. why the founder understands the problem;
4. what has been built since November 2025;
5. what proof is being pursued next.

Avoid exaggerated claims and long feature lists.

### Product demo

Prepare a 2–3 minute demo that shows real working software:

1. finished music/project context;
2. planning/registration/delivery-preparation flow;
3. one differentiated business workflow;
4. remote or cross-module continuity if stable;
5. clear beta labeling.

Do not use fake customer metrics or simulated external integrations as if live.

### Pitch deck

Maintain one modular deck with:

1. title / one-line
2. problem
3. solution
4. product/lifecycle
5. why now
6. founder-market fit
7. working product / technical proof
8. market/customer
9. business model
10. go-to-market
11. current traction truth
12. roadmap / milestones
13. funding ask / use of funds
14. team / hiring plan
15. Detroit story when relevant

### Numbers sheet

One current sheet should contain:

- ownership;
- outside funding;
- revenue/MRR;
- customer/beta counts;
- waitlist;
- pricing;
- current monthly spend;
- founder cash invested;
- funding asks;
- 12/18/24-month budget;
- hiring assumptions.

Never let application agents invent a missing number.

1. do not guess;
2. check repository/current account evidence;
