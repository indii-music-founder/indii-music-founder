## Draft answers

### Describe the business

indii.music is business operating software for independent music artists. It connects the work around finished music — planning, rights and registrations, delivery preparation, creative campaigns, release operations, finance, audience/CRM, merchandise, touring, and recurring business workflows — around shared artist and project context.

### What problem does it solve?

Independent artists increasingly run small businesses without company-grade operating infrastructure. Their work is spread across distributors, rights organizations, spreadsheets, accounting tools, creative tools, social platforms, notes, email, and outside service providers.

The problem is not only the number of tools. It is the loss of context between them.

### What makes it scalable?

indii.music is software. The same cloud/desktop architecture, specialist-agent system, workflow engine, and deterministic business logic can support additional artists without rebuilding the product for each customer.

The system is designed around reusable bounded departments/capabilities rather than one-off consulting labor.

### What stage is the company?

Working software entering founder-led beta validation. The core platform exists. Current work is production hardening, completing external/service connections, real-user onboarding, and commercialization.

The company is pre-revenue and has no paying customers yet.

### Why Detroit?

The company is being built in Detroit by a founder with deep Detroit music and operating roots.

Detroit is globally important in music and increasingly active in technology entrepreneurship. indii.music can build from both: validate with working artists, create technology jobs/contract work as funding expands, and build music-business infrastructure from Detroit rather than assuming music technology has to come from the coasts.

### Founder background

William Roberts has roughly four decades of experience across music and hospitality/operations.

His music background includes DJing, promotion, event production, nightlife operation, production, label ownership, mastering, and hands-on release administration through Expressway Records.

He completed a two-year Music Business program at SAE Institute Nashville.

Since November 2025, indii.music has been his full-time daily focus.

### How does the company use automation?

The product and company are being designed around a low-overhead operating architecture:

automation + bounded specialist AI agents + deterministic software + human approval gates.

A new Living Execution Graph is being designed to connect issue provenance, dependencies, GitHub engineering evidence, discovered work, verification, and outcomes.

The intended company loop is:

Sense → Understand → Decide → Execute → Verify → Learn.

This is an operating-efficiency strategy, not a claim that the company is already fully autonomous.
