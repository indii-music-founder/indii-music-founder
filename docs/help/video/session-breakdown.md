# Session Breakdown: Turn One Long Recording Into Usable Content

**Availability:** Upcoming. Session Breakdown is planned for indii and is not available in the app yet.

## What it will help you do

You will be able to upload one long phone recording and turn it into organized content without losing the original footage. That recording might include a performance, several tries at a show announcement, backstage conversation, a false start, a funny mistake, or a moment you want to save for later.

indii will help you find the useful parts, but you will choose what gets kept.

## What to have ready

- The original phone video.
- The real master recording if you were performing or lip-syncing to one of your songs.
- A rough idea of what you want to make: a performance clip, a show announcement, behind-the-scenes content, a short teaser, or several options.

## How the planned workflow will work

1. Upload your recording. indii keeps the original private and makes a separate version for editing.
2. If your song is playing in the recording, choose the matching master from your catalog.
3. indii checks whether the phone audio and the master line up. It may find a match, ask you to review one, or tell you it cannot safely match them.
4. indii organizes the recording into possible takes, performances, spoken clips, candid moments, setup, and optional bloopers.
5. Review the suggestions. You can keep, reject, trim, or change the choice.
6. Choose how the audio should feel. A performance can use the clean master, some room sound, or a blend. A spoken clip can be cleaned and may use your own music quietly underneath.
7. Approve the selections you want. Only then will indii build an editable project and later create a private output.

## Matching a performance to your master

If you were lip-syncing or performing while your music played in the room, indii will use the phone's recorded audio as a guide and your real master as the clean soundtrack.

That helps different takes line up naturally with the same song. You can use the best moment from more than one take while keeping the performance in time with the master.

Sometimes indii will need your help. It may ask you to choose between a demo and the final version, confirm a repeated chorus, or make a small adjustment. It will not force a weak match.

## Cleaning spoken clips

For announcements, studio updates, or talking-to-camera clips, the planned options will focus on keeping your voice natural. They may reduce rumble, hum, uneven volume, or light background noise.

If the recording is badly clipped, very windy, distant, or covered by loud music, indii should tell you instead of pretending it fixed everything. You can use another take, add captions, use the clip as B-roll, or record a voice-over.

## You stay in control

- Your original video and master are not changed.
- A false start can be rejected, kept, or saved as a blooper.
- You can change a suggested cut before approval.
- A completed analysis does not post anything.
- A completed render does not post anything.
- Posting or scheduling always requires a separate decision.

## If something needs review

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| “Match needs review” | More than one song version may fit, or the timing is uncertain | Pick the right master or adjust the match before continuing |
| “No match found” | indii cannot safely connect the phone audio to a master | Use the clip as spoken content, silent B-roll, or edit it manually |
| “Audio quality needs attention” | Cleanup may not sound natural | Try another take, use captions, record a voice-over, or keep the original sound |
| “Review is out of date” | The source or plan changed after you began reviewing | Review the new version before approving it |

## Privacy and rights

Your footage, masters, and working outputs are intended to stay private to your project. Matching a song does not prove that you own every right connected to it, so indii will not treat a match as rights clearance.

## What is not part of this feature

Session Breakdown is not music generation, automatic publishing, or a promise that every phone recording can be repaired perfectly. It is a way to organize your real footage and your own music into an edit you can understand and approve.
