# Distribution & DSP Relationship Status

**Canonical date:** 2026-09-26  
**Status:** Current truth for fundraising/diligence

## Executive summary

indii.music has substantial distribution-preparation infrastructure and DDEX-oriented engineering, including metadata validation, identifier work, ERN generation, delivery packaging, migration/import work, and pre-flight checks.

Direct-to-DSP production delivery is not currently proven. External messaging should describe the product as preparing releases and building infrastructure required for compliant delivery, not as a live distributor to Spotify, Apple Music, TIDAL, Amazon, Deezer, or other DSPs.

## Repository-supported capability

Current repository evidence supports DDEX ERN 4.3 builders, distribution metadata/readiness validation, DDEX pre-flight evaluation, registration/identifier workflows, deterministic distribution execution code, migration/import concepts, acknowledgement/ingestion components, and a post-mastering administrative engine that audits metadata, identifiers, splits, and PRO/IPI completeness.

These are meaningful product assets. They are not proof of a commercial DSP agreement or production transmission.

## Retired claims

Do not reuse historical draft claims of thousands of artists, millions of streams, DSP-generated revenue, verified partner credentials, live direct delivery, guaranteed lower fees, faster payouts, rejection rates, or delivery SLAs. Those are not current verified business facts.

## Evidence required to upgrade a route

A route becomes externally claimable only after documented onboarding/agreement, valid production credentials, genuine production or sanctioned partner-test transmission, external acknowledgement/receipt, resulting release/reporting visibility, and settlement/reporting evidence where applicable.

Until then, use delivery preparation, DDEX readiness, distribution infrastructure, or migration/interoperability tooling.
