# Valuation Thesis — Historical Worksheet Withdrawn

**As of:** 2026-09-26  
**Status:** Not a current valuation claim

The prior version of this file was an acquisition-oriented scenario worksheet. It combined replacement-cost estimates, test fixtures, simulated commercial data, projected revenue, speculative comparable-company values, and unverified direct-distribution assumptions.

It is **withdrawn as current diligence material**.

## Current truth

indii.music is:

- pre-revenue;
- bootstrapped;
- 100% founder-owned;
- at $0 outside capital reported;
- at 0 paying customers;
- at 0 confirmed active external beta users;
- technically substantial but still entering genuine market validation.

The codebase, architecture, automated tests, security controls, domain-specific systems, and founder-created AI development procedures are real assets. They do not by themselves establish a market valuation.

## What may be used externally

Use evidence-backed descriptions of:

- current product architecture;
- implemented capabilities;
- exact-SHA CI / test evidence;
- IP ownership;
- founder-market fit;
- development velocity;
- beta status;
- current pricing decisions;
- current actual revenue/customer/funding metrics.

## What must not be reused as fact

Do not reuse historical statements asserting:

- a $3M–$7M current valuation;
- a $4.5M headline valuation;
- millions of live streams;
- DSP revenue;
- thousands of active artists;
- direct DSP relationships as commercially live;
- a 17-agent or other fixed fleet count without current verification;
- production-ready external integrations without genuine external verification;
- replacement-cost estimates as company value;
- speculative acquisition comparables as an independent valuation.

## Financing posture

For current fundraising, the useful question is not “what did an internal worksheet once say the company was worth?”

The useful questions are:

1. What milestone is being financed?
2. How much runway does the company require?
3. What evidence will that capital create?
4. What ownership and financing terms are appropriate for the investor/program?

The current working capital ladder is maintained in `09_FINANCIALS_REVENUE_SUMMARY.md` and `../funding/APPLICATION_SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md`.

A formal valuation should be established from the actual financing process, current market evidence, and professional advice where appropriate — not from this historical worksheet.
