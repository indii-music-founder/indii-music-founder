## Official sources inspected on 2026-11-13

- ASCAP official explainer: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WaZQ2SryJW4 — writer signup and simultaneous writer/publisher signup are described as free. Direct ASCAP join/help pages returned 403 during retrieval. To avoid turning that historical explainer into a current fee guarantee, UI directs artists to current official application terms: https://www.ascap.com/join .
- BMI join page: https://www.bmi.com/join — separate writer and publisher application requirements; publisher application lists a processing fee. UI does not default the artist to a society or guarantee terms.
- SoundExchange FAQ: https://www.soundexchange.com/frequently-asked-questions/ — recording royalties differ from PRO composition royalties; registration is free; unclaimed royalties can expire after three years; payment depends on account completion, holds and thresholds. Role selection alone does not establish ownership or guarantee payout.
- The MLC membership: https://www.themlc.com/membership — membership covers those entitled to administer digital audio mechanical rights, including fully/partially self-administered writers and publishers.
- The MLC Get Started: https://www.themlc.com/get-started — distinguishes Member Hub and Songwriter Hub. Existing publisher/administrator mandates must be checked before duplicate claims.
