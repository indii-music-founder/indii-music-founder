# Never overclaim

- Do not say there are no competitors; the stack and inertia are competitors.
- Do not say direct distribution is live without partner-accepted production evidence.
- Do not call configured prices collected revenue.
- Do not describe founder cash as ARR.
- Do not claim security/compliance or complete IP ownership from code and internal documents alone.
- Do not invent churn, CAC, margin, retention, partnerships, or customers.
- Do not call the company worth exactly $20M. It is a target supported only after the required economics and diligence gates exist.
- Say whether a capability is implemented, locally reproduced, production-proven, or externally accepted.
