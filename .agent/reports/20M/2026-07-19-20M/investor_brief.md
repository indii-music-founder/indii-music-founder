# indii investor brief

indii is a purpose-built operating system for independent music businesses, designed to connect creative production, canonical audio, release preparation, rights, finance, marketing, and specialist assistance without forcing artists to reconcile a generic tool stack.

The first customer is a serious independent artist or small team releasing repeatedly. The thesis is a round peg for the music-business round hole: less setup, integration, context switching, and rights/release error. Those outcome claims require real cohort measurement.

The repository demonstrates a broad product and backend head start. Commercial facts—paying cohorts, revenue quality, partner acceptance, full production workflows, legal/IP transferability, and second-operator readiness—must be verified independently before buyer or investor diligence.

Founder cases are $25,000, $50,000, and $37,500 one-time gross cash. They are never ARR and carry lifetime obligations. The $20M central path is $4M–$5M quality ARR plus strong retention, gross margin, efficient acquisition, clean transferability, and operational independence.
