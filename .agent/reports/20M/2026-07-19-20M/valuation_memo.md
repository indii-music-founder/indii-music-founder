# 20M provisional valuation memo

## Decision summary

**As of:** July 19, 2026, `main` at `30caf80e3`. This restored pack preserves the prior planning framework, but the current and remediated valuation ranges require a fresh post-merge technical and commercial audit before external use.

The prior working hypotheses were **$200K–$650K current pre-user value** and **$450K–$1.10M remediated pre-user value**. Confidence is low because pre-revenue value is governed by transferable asset evidence, completion cost, security/legal/IP risk, buyer integration, and founder dependence—not feature count. These ranges are not re-certified against the refreshed mainline.

Founder scenarios cross-foot to $25,000, $50,000, and $37,500 one-time gross cash. Founder receipts are not ARR or MRR and must be reduced for fees, refunds, tax, fulfillment, and lifetime obligations.

## Remediation evidence gate

Remediated means: secure server-authoritative entitlement/cost controls; an authenticated signup/payment-to-flagship-outcome production journey; reproducible upload/audio, creative, rights/finance, agent, and truthful distribution-boundary evidence; clean tests/build/deployment; legal/IP/domain/vendor transfer records; unit-cost telemetry; and a second operator able to deploy, restore, and ship.

## Pricing thesis

Compare gross point-tool stack price, credibly displaced workflows, vertical-fit value, portability, and fully loaded cost-to-serve separately. Official reference points must be refreshed at run time; examples include [QuickBooks pricing](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/pricing/), [Canva pricing](https://www.canva.com/pricing/), and [DistroKid plans](https://distrokid.com/plan/).

$10 can be a bounded activation experiment with strict projects, storage, AI/media, distribution, and support limits. $19/$29/$49/$79/$99 test progressively more complete professional value. The winner is the tier that produces activation, retention, margin, and manageable support—not the highest sticker price.

## Subscriber and target path

The attached deterministic model shows every requested price/milestone and the acquisition path for each target enterprise value. At 4× ARR, $20M EV requires $5M quality ARR; at 5× it requires $4M. Required customers at $49 are 8,504 (4×) or 6,803 (5×); at $79, 5,275 or 4,220; at $99, 4,209 or 3,368.

Customer-count arithmetic is not valuation. A buyer-quality multiple requires collected recurring revenue, mature cohorts, strong gross margin, low/stable churn, GRR/NRR, efficient CAC/payback, low concentration, clean IP/security/legal transferability, and company operation beyond one founder. Current private SaaS context should be refreshed from sources such as [SaaS Capital](https://www.saas-capital.com/blog-posts/private-saas-company-valuations-multiples/) and current completed-transaction datasets.

## Current versus claimed

Before external circulation, rebuild the A/B/C/D/H ledger at the current commit. Code proves implementation, tests prove only their exercised paths, internal documents are claims, and external records prove commercial acceptance. Direct-delivery, customer, revenue, legal ownership, and margin statements receive zero external credit without their source artifacts.
