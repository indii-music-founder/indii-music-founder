# Report contract

Write: decision summary; proven-vs-claimed ledger; product/transferability table; **IP asset bridge** (owned vs licensed vs customer-controlled vs unknown, evidence, restrictions, and transferability); an **IP answer matrix** (asset/posture, evidence available today, value mechanism, restriction, next proof, and accountable real-world owner); current asset bridge; remediation gate/value bridge; replacement-stack and vertical-fit pricing; subscriber matrix; founder scenarios; target acquisition path; risk/action register; assumptions; dated source ledger.

Investor-room runs also require the one-page brief, short/deep live-answer card, diligence Q&A, evidence ledger, and never-overclaim sheet. The room pack must answer, without hand-waving: what the company owns; what remains customer-controlled; what is licensed or vendor-dependent; what evidence is reviewable today; what prevents transfer; what changed since the prior meeting; and why the resulting posture supports only a cautious value range.

Include: “This is a planning valuation, not an appraisal, fairness opinion, offer, legal opinion, tax advice, or guarantee of a transaction price.”
