INDII THESIS SOUNDTRACK

Drop the final theme into this folder using one of these filenames:

  indii-thesis-theme.mp3
  indii-thesis-theme.m4a
  indii-thesis-theme.wav

The thesis player checks those formats in that order. The track loops
automatically, pauses with the crawl, and stops when the thesis closes.

Recommended delivery:

  - MP3 or M4A for the website
  - 44.1 kHz or 48 kHz stereo
  - A clean loop or a long-form score
  - No important opening transient before 100 ms
  - Keep enough headroom for comfortable browser playback

CREATIVE DIRECTION

Reference energy: late-1990s underground tribal house and extended club mixes.
The target is the dark, propulsive feeling of a packed warehouse—not a
cinematic space score and not glossy festival EDM.

  - 125–126 BPM, four-on-the-floor
  - Long DJ-friendly intro with percussion entering in layers
  - Deep, dry kick and rolling tribal percussion
  - Dark elastic bass groove with a small repeating motif
  - Sparse stabs, filtered tension, and slow pressure-building automation
  - One short, original commanding vocal hook used rhythmically
  - Extended arrangement with deliberate drops and patient rebuilds
  - Raw, physical, hypnotic, sexy, nocturnal, and Detroit-adjacent
  - Modern low-end clarity without sanding off the 1990s club character

Avoid:

  - Copying the melody, lyric, vocal cadence, or arrangement of any reference
  - Orchestral trailer music, cosmic pads, dubstep drops, or EDM supersaws
  - Busy lead melodies that compete with the thesis text
  - A constant vocal performance; the crawl needs space to remain central

GENERATION BRIEF

Create an original extended underground tribal-house track at 126 BPM for a
cinematic website thesis sequence. Begin with a mixable kick-and-percussion
intro, then build a dark elastic bass groove, syncopated toms, restrained
metallic hats, filtered chord stabs, and a short commanding original vocal
hook. Keep the arrangement hypnotic and spacious while increasing pressure in
long arcs. Include a clean breakdown and a harder final return. Aim for the
physical urgency of a late-1990s warehouse club record with modern low-end
definition. Do not imitate any existing melody, lyric, vocal performance, or
recording. Deliver an extended 6–8 minute mix with a seamless loop-safe tail.
