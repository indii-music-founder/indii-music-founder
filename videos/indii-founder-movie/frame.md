# frame.md — design truth (indii founder's extended movie)

## Palette

- bg: `#0B0C0F` (near-black, warm-tinted)
- fg: `#F4EFE4` (warm off-white)
- accent: `#F5B13D` (amber — the one accent hue)
- dim: `#6E6A61` (muted warm gray for labels)

## Type

- Display: **Archivo Black** (400) — headlines, tracking -0.03em
- Labels/data: **Space Mono** (400/700) — the machine voice, uppercase, tracking +0.08em
- Headline min 90px; labels min 24px; body min 32px.

## Background layers (2–5 per scene)

- Radial amber glow (low opacity, slow drift)
- Ghost text (theme word at 4% opacity, very large)
- Hairline grid or waveform stroke
- Vinyl-groove rings (Act III)

## Motion

- Film current: LEFT. Seams: cut-the-curve LEFT (stamped from ledger.json).
- Entrances: waterfall entry (binary opacity, power4.out) for headlines;
  staggered card cascades; count-ups for stats.
- Act III close: inverse-zoom arrival (scale 1.25→1, blur 18→0) on the wordmark lockup.
- No idle wobble: every scene's dwell owns a staged reveal, count, or camera move.

## Concept angle

An independent artist's single night, told in three acts — the midnight grind,
the machine that runs the business, and the release the world hears. Amber is
the streetlight that never goes out.
