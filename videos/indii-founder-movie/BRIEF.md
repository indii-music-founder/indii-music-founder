## Intent

The founder's show-off extended movie: three freshly authored scenes stitched
into one continuous 16:9 film. Dark cinematic indii brand piece — the story of
an independent artist's night: the grind, the machine, the release. Heavy
kinetic typography, amber on near-black, a real music bed. Built locally in
HyperFrames (no Veo / cost reservation involved).

## Assets

- docs/assets/indii-wordmark.svg — official indii wordmark; closes Act III.
- test-fixtures/audio/Fading Echoes ext v2.2.mp3 — music bed (97.7s covers 78s).

## Customizations

- Count-up on the stats rows in Act II.
- Inverse-zoom arrival on the wordmark lockup at the close.

## Notes

- Three acts, each ~26s: Act I "THE GRIND" (0–26), Act II "THE MACHINE" (26–52), Act III "THE RELEASE" (52–78).
- Current direction LEFT; seams are cut-the-curve LEFT at t=26 and t=52 (stamped from ledger.json).
- Typography: Archivo Black (display) + Space Mono (labels/data). One accent: amber.
- No stock imagery; all visuals are typography, glows, grids, and vectors.
