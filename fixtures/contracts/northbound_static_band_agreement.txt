BAND CO-WRITING, MASTER OWNERSHIP, AND ASSET SEPARATION AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made effective as of March 1, 2026, by and among the members of the musical group professionally known as "NORTHBOUND STATIC" (hereinafter referred to as the "Band"):

1. MEMBERS AND INDIVIDUAL ROLES
- Leo Vance (Lead Vocals, Lead Guitar, Lyricist)
- Marcus Flint (Drums, Percussion)
- Samira Ray (Bass Guitar, Backing Vocals)
- Tyler Cole (Rhythm Guitar, Synthesizers, Ableton Live Integration)

2. COMPOSITION AND PUBLISHING SPLITS
All musical compositions written during the active existence of Northbound Static shall have songwriting/publishing splits allocated as follows:
- Leo Vance: 35% (Thirty-Five Percent)
- Marcus Flint: 25% (Twenty-Five Percent)
- Samira Ray: 20% (Twenty Percent)
- Tyler Cole: 20% (Twenty Percent)

3. SOUND RECORDING (MASTER) OWNERSHIP
All master recordings funded or co-created by the Band shall be owned in equal shares:
- 25% (Twenty-Five Percent) each to Leo Vance, Marcus Flint, Samira Ray, and Tyler Cole.
No individual member may grant an exclusive sync license or sample clearance without written majority approval (at least 3 of 4 members).

4. SHARED AND INDIVIDUAL EQUIPMENT
- Band-Owned Gear: PA system, drum mics, trailer, and shared Ableton Live license purchased from band funds.
- Individually-Owned Gear: Fender Twin Reverb amplifier (Leo Vance), Vintage Ludwig drum kit (Marcus Flint), Ampeg SVT rig (Samira Ray), Prophet-6 synthesizer (Tyler Cole).

5. EXPENSES AND TOUR REVENUE WATERFALL
Gross performance guarantees and tour revenues shall be disbursed in the following order:
a) Recoupment of documented venue travel, fuel, and hotel expenses.
b) Maintenance of band-owned gear and replacement supplies (strings, sticks, tubes).
c) Equal distribution of remaining net profits to all four members.

6. DISSOLUTION AND DEPARTURE TERMS
In the event of a member departure or full dissolution of Northbound Static:
a) Catalogue rights and master royalty streams shall remain in perpetuity according to the agreed splits in Sections 2 and 3.
b) Individually-owned gear must be returned to the respective owner within 14 business days.
c) Band name and trademark "Northbound Static" may only be used by mutual unanimous agreement.
